Support

For bug reports, suggestions or questions, send an email to atalaycitakdev@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I play?

Drag anywhere to aim. Touching the screen holds your breath and steadies your sights, but hold it too long and your hands start to shake. Lift your finger to fire. A duel has three stages: Precision (ring target), Moving (targets on rails) and Rapid (pop-ups, plates and bottles). Don't shoot the civilian targets.

I bought something but don't have it

Open Settings › RESTORE PURCHASES in the game. Guns and Legendary items come back on any device signed in to the same Apple Account. Gem packs are added once, when the purchase completes.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved only on your device, so it does not move to a new device and is deleted with the app. Purchased items come back with RESTORE PURCHASES.

There is no sound

Check that SOUND is ON in the game's Settings and that your media volume is up.

Are my rivals real players?

No. Range Duel plays fully offline; every rival is a computer-controlled opponent matched to your skill.

How do crates work?

Crates are earned by winning duels and are never sold. Tap a crate to see its drop odds.
