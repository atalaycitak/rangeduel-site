Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Summary

Range Duel has no account and we do not run our own servers. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob between matches; the ad provider collects some device data to show and measure those ads (see Advertising). Buying Remove Ads turns ads off. We do not sell your data and we use no analytics tools.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (trophies, rank, coins, gems, unlocked items, quests) and settings (sound, haptics, aim sensitivity) are saved only on your device. None of this is sent to us, and all of it is removed when you delete the app.

AI opponents

Range Duel plays fully offline. Every rival in a duel is a computer-controlled opponent run on your device; there is no online multiplayer and no Game Center.

Advertising

After every second ranked match the app may show a full-screen ad provided by Google AdMob. To serve, limit and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt), IP address, coarse location derived from it, device and app information, and how you interact with ads. In the EU, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first and can change it any time in Settings → Ad Choices. You can refuse tracking in the iOS prompt or later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; you will then see non-personalized ads. Google processes this data under the Google partner policy and Google Privacy Policy. After you buy Remove Ads, the ad SDK is no longer started.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. The app checks Apple's signed purchase records on your device to unlock what you bought; nothing is sent to us. This data is processed by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy.

Children

Range Duel is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page before that app version is released. For questions, please see the Support page.
